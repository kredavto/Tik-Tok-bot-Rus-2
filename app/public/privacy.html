Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tik_Tok_Loader handles personal data when you use the Service.

1. Data we process

We process Telegram account identifiers and available profile details; your accepted terms and settings; TikTok Open ID, display name, granted scopes, and encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens; videos, captions, hashtags, selected publication settings, and processing status; plan, usage, subscription, and payment confirmation data; and security, webhook, audit, and diagnostic records.

2. Why we process data

We use this data to register and authenticate users, connect TikTok accounts, validate and publish user-confirmed videos, enforce plan limits, process and reconcile payments, notify users, prevent abuse, secure and diagnose the Service, comply with legal obligations, and maintain necessary financial and audit records.

3. TikTok data

TikTok access is limited to the scopes shown on TikTok’s OAuth consent screen. Tokens are encrypted at rest and are not displayed in logs or administrative interfaces. We use TikTok data only to provide user-requested account connection and publishing functions. You can revoke access in the Service or through TikTok’s settings.

4. Sharing and service providers

Data is shared only as needed with Telegram for bot communication and Stars payments, TikTok for OAuth and content posting, payment providers selected in the bot, and infrastructure providers that host and protect the Service. We do not sell personal data or use TikTok data for advertising.

5. Storage and retention

Service data is hosted on protected infrastructure in the Netherlands. Temporary video files are deleted after processing or after the configured retention period. TikTok tokens are removed when an account is disconnected. Payment and audit records are retained where needed for accounting, security, dispute handling, and legal compliance. Log, backup, and temporary-file retention periods are controlled by service policy.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, token encryption, access controls, least-privilege service accounts, webhook signature validation, rate limiting, audit logs, container isolation, backups, and secret management outside the source repository. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may disconnect TikTok, stop using the Service, and request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of eligible personal data. Some payment, security, or audit records may be retained when required by law or legitimate operational needs. We may need to verify the request through your Telegram account.

8. International processing

Telegram, TikTok, payment providers, and infrastructure providers may process data in different countries under their own terms and safeguards. By requesting their functions, you instruct the Service to transmit the data necessary to complete that request.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone who is below the minimum age required to use Telegram or TikTok in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from an ineligible child.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Service, law, or platform requirements change. Questions and privacy requests can be sent through Telegram to @loaderacrdigital_bot.
